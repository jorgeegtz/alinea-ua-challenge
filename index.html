QUESTION 1 · ACCOUNT ARCHITECTURE & SIGNAL MATH

1. Account Structure & Optimization Events

How we structure the account from Day 1 cold start to scaled CBOs, and the mathematical proof behind our optimization event choices.

Why Optimizing for Purchase at Launch Fails (Dual Failure Mode)

Optimizing a brand-new account for Purchase ($120 CAC) on Day 1 fails for two distinct mathematical reasons:

  1. Signal Starvation (Volume Math): At $100,000/month ($23,333/week) and $120 CAC, the entire account only generates 194 Purchases/week in steady state. Split across iOS, Android, Scaling, and Testing, individual ad sets receive only 10–35 events/week—trapping campaigns permanently in Learning Limited (<50 events/week).
  2. The 7-Day Free Trial vs. 7DC Attribution Cliff (Latency Math): Meta’s maximum click attribution window is 7-Day Click (7DC). Because Alinea has a 7-day free trial, any user who clicks an ad on Day 0, signs up, and starts their trial even 4–12 hours later converts to Paid on Day 7.2 to Day 8.5—outside Meta’s 7-day click window! During Week 1 (Days 1–7), Meta sees nearly zero Purchases, causing bids and CPMs to spiral.

Why Trial Start ($48 CPT) is our Primary Scaling Anchor

By anchoring Core Scaling on Trial Start (after a 4-day cold-start warmup on Bank Linked):

  • 2.5× Signal Density: At 40% Trial→Paid CVR, a $120 CAC equals a $48.00 Target Cost per Trial Start (CPT). To hit 50 events/week, an Ad Set needs 50 × $48 = $2,400/week ($343/day) instead of $6,000/week ($857/day).
  • Same-Day Signal Velocity (Zero 7-Day Cliff): 94%+ of Trial Starts occur within 0–24 hours of install. Both Meta’s 7DC1DV pixel/CAPI and Apple’s SKAN 4.0 Window 1 (0–48h fine conversion value) capture the event immediately.
  • High Down-Funnel Intent: Unlike Sign Up ($14.40 CPA, where Meta hunts for cheap email submitters who never link a bank), a Trial Start requires passing KYC/Plaid linkage and committing a payment method.

Optimization Event Progression & Exact Switch Triggers

PHASE 1 · DAYS 1–5 (COLD PIXEL WARMUP)

Scale: Bank Linked ($28.80) · Test: Sign Up ($14.40)

Why: A brand-new pixel has zero conversion weights. Optimizing Scaling for Bank Linked generates ~116 events/day across the account while immediately filtering out low-intent "email-only" leads. Testing uses Sign Up for rapid 48-hour hook reads.

Switch Trigger: Move Scaling to Trial Start as soon as each Scaling Ad Set logs ≥35 Bank Links & ≥15 Trial Starts (typically Day 4–5).
PHASE 2 · DAYS 6–21 (PRIMARY SCALE ENGINE)

Scale: Trial Start ($48.00) · Test: Bank Linked ($28.80)

Why: At $5,833/wk per iOS Scaling Ad Set and $4,667/wk on Android, each Scaling Ad Set generates 97 to 121 Trial Starts/week—More than 2× the 50/week requirement to exit Learning Phase, with same-day CAPI/SKAN feedback.

Switch Trigger: Hold throughout Month 1 unless a single Scaling Ad Set scales past $6,000/wk ($857/day) AND synthetic CAPI value scoring is live.
PHASE 3 · DAY 22+ / MONTH 2 ($150K+ SCALE)

Scale: Funded Trial (Trial + Deposit) or Synthetic pLTV

Why: Because raw Purchase occurs 7 days after Trial Start (falling on the edge of 7DC), we do not blindly switch to delayed Day-8 Purchase. Instead, we fire a custom CAPI event Qualified_Trial_Funded (User starts trial + initiates Bank Deposit within 24h, $73.85 CPA, 61.5% Paid CVR) or run a 50/50 split test once Ad Set budget exceeds $857/day.

Switch Trigger: Ad Set Spend ≥$6,000/wk + ≥50 Funded Trials/wk per Ad Set.
QUESTION 2 · CREATIVE VELOCITY & GRADUATION SYSTEM

2. Creative Testing & Scaling System (50 New Ads / Week)

How we test 10 new concepts × 5 variations (50 ads/week) on $7,000/week without fragmenting signal or burning budget on losers.

How We Structure 50 Ads/Week ($7,000/Wk Testing Budget)

If you put 50 ads into 1 CBO, Meta spends 80% of the budget on 2 high-CTR ads and starves the other 48. Conversely, if you create 50 separate Ad Sets, each gets $140/week (<3 Trial Starts/week—pure noise). Our Concept-Clustered ABO System solves both:

  • 10 Concept Batches / Week ($700 Allocated per Concept): Each concept has 1 core body/script and 4–6 hook/visual variations (e.g., 3 video hooks + 1 static + 1 carousel/format variant).
  • 2x Weekly Launch Cadence (Tuesday & Friday Drops):
    • Batch A (Tuesday, 5 Concepts = 25 Ads): $3,500 budget over 72–96 hours across 3 iOS + 2 Android Concept ABO Ad Sets.
    • Batch B (Friday, 5 Concepts = 25 Ads): $3,500 budget over 72–96 hours.
  • Why Concept-Level Ad Sets Work: Inside a single Concept Ad Set, all 5 ads share the same core value proposition and compete only on Hook / Thumbstop efficiency. Meta naturally routes spend to the strongest 1–2 hook variations per concept within 48 hours.

4-Stage Automated Stop-Loss & Winner Thresholds

Gate 1: 2,500 Impressions (~$72 Spend) — Hook & CTR Gate

Kill Rule: Outbound CTR < 0.75% OR Video Thumbstop (3s views / Impressions) < 20%.
Diagnosis: Hook failed to stop scroll. If Hold Rate (15s) is ≥10%, salvage body with a new 3-second hook.

Gate 2: $45 Ad Spend (3x Target CPSU) — Upper-Funnel Gate

Kill Rule: 0 Sign Ups at $45 spend, OR Cost per Sign Up (CPSU) > $21.60 (1.5× target $14.40) after $75 spend.

Gate 3: $120 Concept Spend (72 Hours) — Plaid Trust Gate

Kill Rule: Cost per Bank Linked (CPBL) > $40.00 (vs. $28.80 target) OR 0 Trial Starts after $120 spend.
Diagnosis: "Clickbait Trap"—ad drives cheap sign-ups who refuse to link their bank account.

Gate 4: Day 5–7 Graduation Criteria (What Makes a Winner)

Winner Threshold: Concept achieves ≥8–10 Trial Starts at CPT ≤ $48.00 (or CPBL ≤ $28.80 with ≥55% Day-0 Bank Deposit rate) and Outbound CTR ≥ 1.25% over 5–7 days.

1. How Winners Graduate

We never pause the winning ad in Testing while it’s still profitable, and we never re-upload from scratch. We extract the existing Meta Post ID and duplicate it into ALN_US_iOS_Scaling_CBO and ALN_US_AND_Scaling_CBO in Weekly Batches (max 2x/week so we don’t reset CBO learning). Preserves 100% of comments, likes, and algorithmic engagement history.

2. How We Scale Winners (Horizontal + Vertical)

Vertical Scale: Increase Scaling CBO budgets by +15% to +20% every 48–72 hours whenever trailing 3-day CPT ≤ $46 and Funded Trial Ratio ≥ 65%.
Horizontal Scale (The Multiplier): Every graduated winner immediately triggers a "Fast-Follow Iteration Brief": 5 new opening hooks (0–3s), 2 new creator voices, and 9:16 Reels / 4:5 Feed / Static cutdowns.

3. Pulling Back & Creative Fatigue

Fatigue Leading Indicators: 7-day Prospecting Frequency > 2.2x, Thumbstop dropping >20% from peak, and 3-day rolling CPT exceeding $56 (+17% over target).
Action: We don't wait for CAC to hit $150. When CPT crosses $55 for 72h with declining CTR, we rotate in the pre-tested Hook Variants of that concept and demote the fatigued Post ID.

QUESTION 3 · MEASUREMENT & PREDICTIVE COHORT CAC

3. Measurement: Navigating the 7-Day Free Trial Lag

How we know within Days 3–14 whether we are on track for a $120 CAC before 7-day trials finish converting—and which data source governs which decision.

The Week-1 Measurement Trap: Because every new user enters a 7-day free trial before converting to a paid annual subscription, Week 1 Cash Purchases will be near zero (only immediate trial-skippers). A naive marketer looking at Week 1 Spend ($18,000) / Week 1 Cash Purchases (~25) = $720 Apparent CAC will panic and shut off winning campaigns! Instead, we manage Weeks 1–2 using 3 Leading Cohort Indicators (Day-0 / Day-1 Proxies) and Lagged Cohort Maturity Curves.

The Day-1 Predictive CAC Formula (pCAC)

Within 24 hours of install, we predict each cohort’s Day-8 Paid CAC using three deterministic internal + MMP signals:

pCAC = Cohort CPT ÷ [ (Funded_Trial_% × 61.5%) + (Unfunded_NonCanceled_Trial_% × 14.0%) ]
  • Metric 1: Cohort Cost per Trial Start (CPT ≤ $48.00)
    Tracked on Day 0/Day 1. At our baseline 40% Trial→Paid CVR, a $48.00 CPT mathematically locks in a $120.00 Paid CAC ($48 / 0.40 = $120).
  • Metric 2: Day-0 Funded Trial Ratio (Bank Deposit / Trial Start ≥ 65%)
    This is the ultimate FinTech quality filter. A user who starts a free trial AND initiates a bank deposit on Day 0 has skin in the game (61.5% convert to Paid on Day 8). If an ad drives $35 CPTs but only 20% initiate a deposit, it is attracting "freebie hunters"—we kill it on Day 3 without waiting 7 days!
  • Metric 3: 24-Hour Auto-Renew Survival Rate (≥ 82%)
    In subscription apps, ~80% of all trial cancellations happen within the first 24 hours ("subscribe & immediately cancel in Apple Settings"). Measuring 24h survival gives us an accurate Trial-to-Paid read by Day 2 instead of Day 8.
